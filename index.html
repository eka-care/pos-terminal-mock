POS Terminal Mock

A stand-in card machine, for developers who do not have one.

Integrating an Indian POS terminal means writing code against hardware you probably cannot keep on your desk, and against API docs that do not always match what the device does. This is a local server that answers the terminal's cloud API the way the real one answered it, so you can build the whole flow before you ever hold a machine. 

npx github:eka-care/pos-terminal-mock

Point your integration's provider host at http://localhost:8201 - that is the only change. Then open that page and you are the customer at the counter.

Why it exists

  • The sale resolves later, and maybe elsewhere. The machine does not call you back. You learn what happened by asking, and the thing you must handle is a sale whose asker went away.
  • The failures are the interesting part. A declined card, a customer who walks off mid-payment, a sale nobody completes, a cancel that arrives too late. You cannot produce those on demand with real hardware, and you certainly cannot produce them in CI.
  • The docs and the device disagree. Several behaviours were found with a physical terminal on a UAT account, and they are the ones that break integrations.

The behaviours that bite

  • Every reply is HTTP 200. The outcome lives in ResponseCode.
  • A non-numeric SequenceNumber is rejected with HTTP 400, before any credential check.
  • AllowedPaymentMode is pipe-separated (1|10); a comma list is refused.
  • Re-uploading the same TransactionNumber replaces the live sale with a new reference, so a blind retry orphans the sale you were tracking.
  • A pending sale reads as failed on more than one adapter, which false-declines a card that is about to go through.

The full contract, and which behaviours were observed on a device as against assumed, is in docs/pine-labs.md.
